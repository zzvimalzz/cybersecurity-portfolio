## Repository layout

- `index.html`: home page with all seven portfolio sections
- `_data/site.yml` and `_data/skills.yml`: the content of the home page
- `projects/<name>/README.md`: write-up for each project
- `projects/<name>/evidence/`: sanitised screenshots, diagrams, reports and scripts
- `assets/`: stylesheet, script, fonts and images

## Safety and privacy

All work targets my own lab, legal practice platforms or systems I am authorised to test. No credentials, client names, phone numbers or home addresses are published here.
